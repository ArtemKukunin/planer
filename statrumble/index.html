Stat Rumble

Stat Rumble publishes short bar chart race videos that show how countries rank over time on public World Bank indicators: GDP, population, energy, health, education and more.

Stat Rumble Publisher is a private tool used only by the channel owner to upload and schedule the channel's own videos on YouTube, Facebook and Instagram.
