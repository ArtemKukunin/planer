Privacy Policy

Applies to: Stat Rumble Publisher (Google / YouTube) and BIMBA Reels (Meta: Facebook and Instagram). Last updated: 2 November 2026.

These are personal tools operated by Artem Kukunin, the owner of the Stat Rumble and BIMBA News pages. They are not offered to the public and have no other users.

What the apps access

With the owner's explicit consent, the apps upload, schedule and publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts, and read basic information about those accounts (names, IDs, publishing status). They do not access other people's accounts, messages or personal data.

What is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer in a private folder. No personal data of viewers, followers or other people is collected, stored, sold or shared.

Use of Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data deletion and revoking access

The apps keep no user data on any server. To remove access, revoke it at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) or in Facebook Settings → Business integrations / Apps and websites (Meta). To request deletion of any data, email artemkukunin96@gmail.com — it will be deleted within 30 days.
