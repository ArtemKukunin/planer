Privacy Policy — Stat Rumble Publisher

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Stat Rumble Publisher is a personal tool operated by the owner of the Stat Rumble channels. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

With the owner's explicit consent, the app uses the YouTube Data API to read basic information about the owner's own Stat Rumble channel and to upload and schedule the channel's own videos. It does not access other users' accounts or data.

What is stored

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's computer in a private folder. No personal data of viewers or other people is collected, sold or shared.

Use of Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
